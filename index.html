I am a Ghent-based independent researcher specializing in Anglophone new weird fiction, with a focus on form and affect. I received my Master’s degree in English and Swedish literature and linguistics from Ghent University in 2025, as well as an Advanced Master’s degree in literary studies from KU Leuven in 2026.

I have previously worked as a teaching assistant (“onderwijsbegeleider”) at Ghent University, where I was responsible for supervising, evaluating, grading the written assignments for the undergraduate course "English Literature: Overview 1800 to Present." As a student, I was involved with the Ghent University PALs, where I was part of the steering committee for two years and served as a writing mentor for three years.

My research has featured at the "Metamorphoses in Contemporary Literature" conference, hosted at the Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz. You can request both of my master’s theses via my ResearchGate profile. Independently published essays can be found on this website on the “Blog” page.
